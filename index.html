Sources and classifications

The stellar assignments follow Butler’s catalogue in “World Abounding”: one world around Sol, four around Proxima, six around Alpha, and six around Beta. The catalogue restricts both habitability and the size and distance of the worlds included.

Classifications sometimes differ between works. Aranea is both the Spider Planet and the Spider Asteroid; Proavitus is a big asteroid included among the Alpha worlds. Tarshish is recorded as an aspect of Klepsis. Collective entries are groups, not single celestial bodies.

All 282 citation records have been checked against the supplied texts. Citations identify the work and edition. The Dark Shine references use the supplied edited text, not a collation with the original typescript. Speaker claims, disputed identities, and non-celestial name references are identified where relevant. This verifies the citations; it does not establish that the atlas contains every place in the corpus.
